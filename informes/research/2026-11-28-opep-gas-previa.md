Fuentes: Star Advertiser 1-nov https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/11/01/breaking-news/opec-keeps-december-oil-output-targets-steady-as-iran-war-disrupt-flows/ ; The Star 2-nov https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/11/02/opec-to-maintain-oil-output-for-3rd-consecutive-month-in-december [D].
